Who's actually in there.

Golf, nothing more has been running since 2013. This is what Facebook's own figures say about the people in it — including the parts that don't flatter us.

Age

The middle of the working life

The group skews to the ages where people have both the time and the money for golf. A quarter of members are between 35 and 44, and three in four are 35 or over.

Geography

Four markets carry three quarters of it

More than half the group is in the United States. Melbourne, Sydney and Perth all sit in the top five cities, so the Australian presence is heavier than the country figure suggests.

Straight answers

What we don't know yet

A media kit that only lists its best numbers isn't worth reading. Here is what we can't tell you.

We don't have handicap or spend data

Facebook doesn't collect it and we don't keep the answers people give when they join. A member survey is planned; until it has run, anyone quoting you a handicap profile for this audience is guessing.

Reach is not attention

We've measured posts that reached thousands of people and drew no reaction at all. We sell engagement per post, not impressions, and we'd rather show you a small honest number than a large meaningless one.

Not every member is a customer

Around one in twenty members is outside the shipping and service area of most golf brands. When we quote you an addressable audience, we leave those out.

The email list is new

It started in September 2026. It is small, it is growing, and we will tell you exactly how small it is this week rather than round it up.
