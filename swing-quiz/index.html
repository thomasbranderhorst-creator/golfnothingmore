01  The route

Seven steps, one direction
  1. 1
    Facebook page

    The video goes up on the page first

    A silhouette of a famous swing. Which swing is this? It has to live on the page, because a comment is only worth something to us if it sits under a page post.

  2. 2
    The group

    The page shares it into the group

    Posted as Golf, nothing more, which keeps it on brand. Being group admin is enough to switch the comments off afterwards, so the page can be the author.

  3. 3
    The group

    Comments off on the group post

    Three dots, Turn off comments. Now the only way to answer is to tap through to the page. The first line of the caption has to say exactly that, or people scroll on.

  4. 4
    Facebook page

    They answer under the original

    This is the one comment that counts. Comments inside a group trigger nothing, which is the whole reason for this detour.

  5. 5
    Manychat

    Two things fire at once

    A private message in Messenger, and a public reply under their comment. The public one is the safety net, because new pages land in Message requests.

  6. 6
    Messenger

    The offer is a free swing review

    They just judged someone else's swing. Now a PGA pro judges theirs. No app, no code, they upload a clip and that is it.

  7. 7
    Sandbag

    The answer only plays in the app

    A pro records a voice review. To hear it, they install Sandbag, and almost everyone who uploads gets that far.

03  Before anything goes live

In this order

  • Manychat comment automation built and switched onA comment that arrives before the automation is awake is gone. This comes first, every time.

  • Both channels enabled: private message and public replyFive rotating versions of the public reply, each carrying the link in plain sight, because new pages land in Message requests.

  • Group settings allow pages to postOtherwise the option to post as Golf, nothing more is not there when you need it.

  • Blocked word list on the page switched onSet it before the video goes up, not after the first argument starts. Hide comments rather than delete them: a hidden commenter still gets their link.

  • First frame already shows the hair and the tieFacebook often shows a still before it plays. If the clip opens on the address position the joke is gone before it starts.

  • Tested from an account that is not oursAdmins and page owners do not behave like ordinary members. Your point on the call, and a good one.

  • Your link used as it is, with nothing wrapped around itget.sandbag-golf.com/voice-analyze/gnm goes in raw. Putting one of our own redirects in front of it is exactly what broke the last one inside Messenger.

  • Walked through with you before we publishAs agreed. The worst outcome is a post that works and earns nothing.

04  What can kill it

Known in advance

Shares travel less far

A share reaches fewer people than native video, and switching comments off removes Facebook’s strongest signal.

What we doAsk for a reaction instead. Those still count.

The messages hide

On your new page they landed in Message requests. Ours is under 60 followers, so we assume the same.

What we doThe public reply carries the link too, in plain sight.

Nobody taps through

People reach for the comment box where it always is. When it is gone, most scroll on.

What we doFirst line of the caption says to tap the video to answer.
