# Making the daily deals post work

Notes for Josh, from the group's own numbers
Golf, nothing more · 18 September 2026

---

## Where the posts sit now

Your recent deals posts reached 3,600, 3,100 and 1,800 people. Each shows 8 comments,
and all eight are your own link comments. **No member has replied to any of them.**

The group average across its best-performing posts is **8.5 comments per 1,000 views**.

In the same period you posted 27 times, and none of those posts made the group's
top-posts export. That isn't a judgement on the deals. It's what happens to any post
built as a list.

Facebook decides how far a post travels largely by what people do with it in the first
hour. A list of links gives someone nothing to do except click away, so the post stops
being distributed and the reach settles low. Repeating the same layout daily makes that
worse, because the algorithm damps repetition.

The eight self-posted comments probably work against the post as well. They raise the
comment count without producing any of the signals distribution actually runs on:
replies, reactions on comments, time spent in the thread.

## What the same subject looks like when it works

A member posted this:

> "Anybody switch to JumboMax Grip and it changed their life? I hit my buddy's on the
> range. And was incredibly surprised! It's got me really thinking"

**148 comments. 13,278 views. 11.1 per 1,000.**

Same topic as your posts. Equipment, and whether it's worth buying. Four times the reach, and 148
replies against none.

The difference isn't the product and it isn't the audience. One is a list. The other is
someone thinking out loud and asking what other people reckon.

## What the group actually rewards

Measured against that 8.5 average:

| Type of post | Comments per 1,000 |
|---|---|
| Someone asking which club, or whether to change something | 27.6 |
| "If your driver isn't performing, would you try a new shaft first?" | 59.7 |
| "How far do you hit driver? Honest answer please" | 22.3 |
| Announcements and link round-ups | around 2 |
| The current daily deals post | 0 from members |

One more, worth remembering: a hole-in-one photo in this group got **360,529 views and
79 comments**. Reach is not the same thing as a conversation, and it's the conversation
that carries the next post.

---

## The change

Keep the daily rhythm. Change what's in the post.

**One product, not a list.** Pick the single best deal of the day.

**A real photo of it.** Not a graphic with the date on it. People stop for a product,
not for a header.

**The price, in the post.** "$179, down from $299."

**A question, four or five words.** "Worth it at $179?" · "Anyone actually gaming these?"
· "Overrated or underrated?"

**The rest of the deals in the first comment**, exactly as you do now.

That turns the post from an advert into an opinion thread about a product, which is the
format the group responds to. A fair target for the first month: five replies from people
other than you. The links still get their click, they just arrive after a
conversation instead of instead of one.

---

## When to post

The group has two peaks, and both are US hours:

- **Around 9am Eastern** — the quieter of the two, but a clean run at the morning feed
- **7pm to 10pm Eastern** — the busiest block of the day

The current posting time sits outside both. Moving to the evening block is the single
easiest change on this page.

---

## What not to change

The daily cadence works. Consistency is why people know to look for it.

Links in the first comment is right. Links in the post body get throttled harder.

And the deals themselves are good. That's not the problem being solved here.
