How it works

02

Why the second step matters

The same group answers some posts thirty times more than others

We measure every post in the group by how many comments it draws per 1,000 people who see it. That tells us something most marketers have to guess: how golfers respond to a type of message before you pay to show it to them. It is why we adapt your content before we run it.

The clearest example is our own. Our first post from our page, on 18 September 2026, was an announcement with a link. It reached 250 people. A few days later we posted a photo and a question with nothing to click. It reached 3,300 people in its first hour, and 14,852 people and 101 comments in three days. Same page, same group. Most content that goes out as an announcement could have gone out as a question.
