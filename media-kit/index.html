Who we are

A golf group with a company behind it

The group has been running since 2013 and is one of the largest amateur golf communities anywhere. Since 2026 there is a company behind it, and the reason is simple: a community this size was already being sold to, just not openly and not by us.

We have one rule, and everything else follows from it: members don't sell in here. No affiliate links, no "DM me", no funnels. That rule is what keeps the group worth being in, and it is also why a commercial post in here carries weight: the only ones are ours and those of a few partners we let in.

83% of members are men, and roughly three quarters are 35 or over — the ages where people have both the time and the money for golf. What they shoot we honestly don't know, and we say so further down rather than guess at it here.

Straight answers

What we can't tell you yet

How big the email list is. It started in September 2026. It is small. Write to us and we'll tell you the number that week rather than round it up — and if it's too small to be worth your time, we'll say so.

What a paid post returns. Our first post from our own page, on 18 September, was an announcement with a link in it. It reached 250 people. A few days later we posted a photo with a question and nothing to click. It reached 3,300 people in its first hour and took 19 comments. Same page, same group. In here, a post that sends people away does not travel, and a post that gives them something to do does. The $550 is an introductory rate, not a rate we can yet defend with numbers, and we'd rather say that than dress it up.

What our members play and what they spend. Facebook doesn't collect it and we don't keep the answers people give when they join. A member survey is planned. Anyone quoting you a handicap profile for an audience this size is guessing.

One in twenty members is outside the shipping area of most golf brands. When we quote you an addressable audience, we leave those out.
